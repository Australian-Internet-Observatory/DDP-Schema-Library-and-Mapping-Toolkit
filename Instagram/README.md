
#  Instagram
Instagram allows users to download personal data collected from their accounts, including posts, messages, and interactions.
## Accessing Digital Data Packages (DDPs)
**Steps:**
1. Go to **Settings & Privacy → Settings**
2. Open **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions**
3. Navigate to **Your Instagram Information**
4. Click **Download Your Information**
5. Choose the data types, file format (**JSON** or **HTML**), and date range
6. Click **Create File**  
   Meta will prepare your data package and send a notification once it’s ready.
7. You have **4 days** to download your file after notification.

 **More info:** [Meta Help Center](https://help.instagram.com)

The Instagram DDP is provided as a downloadable .zip archive that is systematically organized into multiple folders and subfolders. Upon submitting a data request, users receive this archive, which contains various JSON and HTML files. The dataset encompasses comprehensive information about the user’s account activity, shared content, and interactions on the platform.

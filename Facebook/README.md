
#  Facebook

Facebook allows users to download the personal data collected from their accounts, including posts, messages, interactions, and more. 

## How to Download Your Facebook Data

To download your data from Facebook, follow these steps:

1. Go to **Settings & Privacy > Settings**.
2. Select **Accounts Center > Your information and permissions**.
3. Navigate to **Your Facebook Information**.
4. Click **Download Your Information**.
5. Choose the types of data you want to include.
6. Select:
   - **Date range**
   - **File format** (JSON or HTML)
   - **Media quality** (photos and videos)
7. Click **Create File**. Facebook will prepare the data package.
8. You will receive a notification when the file is ready.

The Facebook DDP is provided as a downloadable .zip archive that is systematically organized into multiple folders and subfolders. Upon submitting a data request, users receive this archive, which contains various JSON or HTML files.

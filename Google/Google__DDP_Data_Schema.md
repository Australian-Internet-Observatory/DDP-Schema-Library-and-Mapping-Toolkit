# Google Services DDP data schema 

The data downloaded from Google Takeout is provided as a ZIP file containing information from the applications selected by the user. Out of ninety-six available applications, data from nine was chosen for this analysis. Since Google offers a wide range of services, only a subset relevant to the research objectives and analysis of user behavior was included. Information related to personal communications and utilities, such as alarms, reminders, call records, phone calls, email, and Google Wallet, was excluded.

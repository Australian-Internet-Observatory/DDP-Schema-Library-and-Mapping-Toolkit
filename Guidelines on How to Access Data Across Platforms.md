# Accessing Digital Data Packages (DDPs)

This repository provides clear, platform-specific instructions on how to access and download **Data Download Packages (DDPs)** from major digital platforms.  
These packages allow users and researchers to retrieve personal data archives for research purposes.

---

##  Instagram
Instagram allows users to download personal data collected from their accounts, including posts, messages, and interactions.

**Steps:**
1. Go to **Settings & Privacy → Settings**
2. Open **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions**
3. Navigate to **Your Instagram Information**
4. Click **Download Your Information**
5. Choose the data types, file format (**JSON** or **HTML**), and date range
6. Click **Create File**  
   Meta will prepare your data package and send a notification once it’s ready.
7. You have **4 days** to download your file after notification.

 **More info:** [Meta Help Center](https://help.instagram.com)

##  Facebook

Facebook allows users to download the personal data it has collected from their accounts, providing access to a comprehensive archive of information including posts, photos, messages, reactions, and more.

**Steps to download your Facebook data:**

1. Go to **Settings & Privacy → Settings** on Facebook.  
2. Open **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions**.  
3. Navigate to **Your Facebook Information**.  
4. Click on **Download Your Information**.  
5. Select the **types of data** you want to download.  
6. Choose the desired **date range**, **file format** (typically **JSON** or **HTML**), and **media quality** (for photos and videos).  
7. Click **Create File**, and Facebook will prepare your data package.  
   You’ll be notified once it’s ready for download.  

 **Note:** For security, users typically have a limited time (about **4 days**) to download their files after receiving the notification.

 **More information:** [Facebook Help Center – Download Your Information](https://www.facebook.com/help/212802592074644)

##  X (formerly known as Twitter)

**X** (formerly known as **Twitter**) is a social media platform where people share short posts called *tweets*, which may include text, photos, videos, links, and polls.  
Users can follow others, like, reply, repost, and use hashtags to join global conversations. The platform is widely used for real-time news, trends, and discussions, allowing individuals, communities, and organizations to connect and exchange ideas quickly.

**Steps to download your X account data:**

1. Go to **Settings and Privacy**.  
2. Click on **Your Account**.  
3. Select **Download an archive of your data**.  
4. Click on **Request Your Data**.  
5. You will receive an **in-app notification** when your data archive is ready to download.  

 The downloaded archive is provided as a **ZIP file**, which contains your account information, activity history, connected apps and devices, interests, and ads data.  

 **More information:** [X Help Center – Download an archive of your data](https://help.twitter.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-download-your-twitter-archive)

## 🟣 TikTok

**TikTok** is a social media platform focused on creating and sharing short-form videos, often set to music, sound effects, or voiceovers.  
Users can record, edit, and enhance videos with filters, effects, and text. The platform recommends content based on user interests and allows interaction via likes, comments, shares, follows, hashtags, and trending sounds.

**Steps to download your TikTok account data:**

1. Go to your **Profile**.  
2. Select **Manage Account**.  
3. Tap **Download TikTok Data**.  
4. Choose the desired download option:
   - **All Data**: Downloads all available information associated with the account.  
   - **Custom Files**: Allows selection of specific data types:
     - **Profile and Posts**: Personal information, list of followers, accounts followed, and user posts.  
     - **Activity**: Likes, comments, favorites, and browsing history.  
     - **Messages**: Direct messages sent and received.  

Once the request is submitted, TikTok processes it and notifies the user when the data is ready. The download must be completed within **4 days** of notification.  

📘 **More information:** [TikTok Data Portability](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/data-protection)

## 🟥 Netflix

**Netflix** is a streaming platform that allows users to watch a wide variety of movies, TV shows, documentaries, and original productions on-demand.  
Subscribers can access content anytime through the Netflix app or website, with options to pause, resume, or download shows for offline viewing. The platform provides personalized recommendations based on viewing history, helping users discover new content.  

**Steps to download your Netflix account data:**

1. Go to **Settings**.  
2. Navigate to **Security → Personal Info Access**.  
3. Click **Request**.  
4. Confirm the request using the **email** sent to the account’s registered address.  
5. Netflix will notify you via email when the data is ready to download.  

📦 The downloaded data is provided as **CSV files** organized in different folders.  

📘 **More information:** [Netflix Help Center – Access Your Personal Information](https://help.netflix.com/en/account-data)

## 🚗 Uber

**Uber** is a ride-hailing platform that connects passengers with drivers through a mobile app.  
Users can request rides, view driver details, estimated fares, and arrival times, and make payments directly through the app. Uber also offers services like **Uber Eats**, carpooling, and premium rides.  

**Steps to download your Uber account data:**

1. Go to [Uber](https://auth.uber.com/v2/).  
2. Log in to your account.  
3. Click on **Download**.  
4. Click **Request Your Data**.  
5. Uber will notify you via email when the data is ready to download.  

📦 The downloaded data is provided as **CSV files**, organized in different folders.  
Data may include personal information, rider activity, and delivery information, depending on how the account has been used.  

📘 **More information:** [Uber Help Center – Request Your Data](https://help.uber.com)

## 🎵 Spotify

**Spotify** is a music and audio streaming platform that allows users to listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and audiobooks worldwide.  
Users can search for tracks, artists, or albums, create and share playlists, and receive personalized recommendations based on listening habits. Spotify offers both **free** (ad-supported) and **premium** (ad-free, offline downloads) options.  

**Steps to download your Spotify account data:**

1. Log in to your **Spotify account**.  
2. Navigate to **Account Settings → Privacy Settings → Download Your Data**.  
3. Select the **data package(s)** you wish to request (you can choose one, multiple, or all).  
4. Spotify will prepare the data. Processing may take **5–30 days** depending on the package.  
5. Users have **14 days** to download the file before it expires.  

📘 **More information:** [Spotify Data Download](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

## 🏡 Airbnb

**Airbnb** is an online platform that allows users to book accommodations and experiences worldwide.  
Users can search for listings, read reviews, book spaces such as apartments, houses, cabins, or unique stays, and participate in experiences offered by local hosts. The platform also allows users to manage stays, reviews, payments, and personal profiles.  

**Steps to download your Airbnb account data:**

1. Go to **Account Settings**.  
2. Navigate to the **Privacy** tab.  
3. Select **Request My Personal Data** under the Data Privacy section.  
4. Choose your **residence** and preferred **data format** (HTML, JSON, or Excel).  
5. Confirm the request using the **verification code** sent to your registered mobile number.  
6. Airbnb will notify you via email when your data is ready to download.  

📦 The downloaded data is provided as a **ZIP file**.  

📘 **More information:** [Airbnb Data Privacy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2851/requesting-a-copy-of-your-personal-data)

## 🟢 Google Services

**Google Services** are a collection of online tools and applications for communication, productivity, entertainment, and information.  
These include **Gmail** (email), **Google Drive** (cloud storage), **Google Maps** (navigation), **Google Photos** (image storage), **Google Calendar** (scheduling), **Google Docs, Sheets, Slides** (document collaboration), **YouTube** (video content), **Google Search**, and **Google Translate**, among others.  

**Steps to download your Google account data using Google Takeout:**

1. Go to [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/).  
2. Sign in with your Google account.  
3. Select the Google services you want to export (or choose all available services).  
4. Choose the preferred **file formats** (e.g., JSON, CSV, MBOX, HTML) and delivery method.  
5. Click **Create Export**.  
6. Google will process your request and notify you when the archive is ready to download.  

📦 The exported files may vary in format depending on the service.  
For research purposes, only a subset of Google products was included to analyze user behavior. Personal communications and utilities (such as alarms, reminders, calls, or Google Wallet) were excluded.  

📘 **More information:** [Google Takeout Help](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190)
